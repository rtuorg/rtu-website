Admissions Overview

Rajasthan Technical University (RTU) offers admissions to Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), and Doctoral (Ph.D.) programs through centralized counselling and entrance examinations.

Eligibility Criteria

  • B.Tech: 10+2 with Physics & Mathematics + JEE Main qualification
  • M.Tech: B.Tech/B.E. + valid GATE score
  • MBA: Graduation + CMAT / Entrance Test
  • MCA: Graduation with Mathematics
  • Ph.D.: Postgraduate degree with required marks

Admission Process (Step-by-Step)

  1. Online Registration through official admission portal
  2. Fill application form with academic details
  3. Upload required documents
  4. Pay application fee
  5. Participate in counselling process
  6. Seat allotment based on merit/rank
  7. Document verification
  8. Final admission confirmation

Required Documents

  • 10th & 12th Marksheet
  • Graduation Certificate (for PG)
  • Entrance Exam Scorecard (JEE/GATE/CMAT)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Migration Certificate
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile Certificate
  • Passport Size Photographs

Important Admission Highlights

  • Admission is merit-based and entrance exam based
  • Centralized counselling is conducted by Rajasthan authorities
  • Lateral entry available for diploma holders
  • Multiple affiliated colleges across Rajasthan

How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official admission portal, complete registration, and follow counselling procedures as per schedule.
