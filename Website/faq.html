Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries

What courses are offered at RTU? ▼
RTU offers B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, MCA and other technical programs.
How can I apply for admission? ▼
Admissions are conducted through centralized counseling and entrance exams.
How to verify my degree certificate? ▼
You can apply through RTU verification portal or submit request to examination department.
What is the exam pattern? ▼
RTU follows internal assessment + end-term examination system.
How can I get transcripts? ▼
You can request transcripts via RTU examination section with required documents.
