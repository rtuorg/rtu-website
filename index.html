About RTU

Rajasthan Technical University (RTU) was established in 2006 by the Government of Rajasthan to enhance technical education in the state. The university affiliates multiple engineering, management, and technical colleges across Rajasthan.

Courses Offered

B.Tech

Engineering programs in multiple disciplines.

M.Tech

Postgraduate engineering programs.

MBA

Management and business administration courses.

MCA

Computer applications and IT programs.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To achieve academic excellence and meet global industrial demands.

Mission:

  • Provide quality technical education
  • Promote research and innovation
  • Develop industry-ready students
